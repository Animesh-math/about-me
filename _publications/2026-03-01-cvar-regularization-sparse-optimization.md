We develop CVaR-based regularization techniques to promote sparsity and measure concentration in optimization problems constrained by PDEs. The analysis uses tools from convex and nonlinear functional analysis — dual representations, optimality conditions — and we implement and test numerical methods for the resulting PDE-constrained problems.

With [H. Antil](https://math.gmu.edu/~hantil/) and B. Mendieta. *(In preparation)*
