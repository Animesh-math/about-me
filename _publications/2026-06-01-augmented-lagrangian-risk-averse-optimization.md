We develop augmented Lagrangian methods for optimization under uncertainty, incorporating risk measures either in the objective or as constraints. We investigate alternative formulations and their numerical performance in PDE-constrained optimal control problems, with an emphasis on computational efficiency by reducing the number of expensive model evaluations.

With [H. Antil](https://math.gmu.edu/~hantil/), Y. Cui, and J. Roth. *(In preparation)*
