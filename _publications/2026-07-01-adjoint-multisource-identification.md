We formulate a direct, nonconvex optimization problem for identifying multiple sources in advection–diffusion systems. An initialization strategy is developed to improve the robustness of the optimization procedure and mitigate sensitivity to local minima. Applications include source identification and optimal actuator placement in complex urban geometries.
