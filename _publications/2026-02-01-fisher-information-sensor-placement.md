We develop a Fisher-information-based framework for optimal sensor placement in structural digital twins. The approach leverages adjoint-based finite element solvers and matrix-free operator formulations for efficient computation of the Fisher information matrix. We derive analytic results for D-optimal sensor configurations — including uniform spacing in a 1D structural model — and validate the framework through benchmarks.

With [H. Antil](https://math.gmu.edu/~hantil/) and R. Löhner. [arXiv:2602.02981](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02981)
